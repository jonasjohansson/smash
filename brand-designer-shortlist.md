# Brand Designer Shortlist

**Purpose:** A backup list of brand identity designers in case Jens falls through for the June engagement. This is *parallel insurance*, not a replacement search. Compiled 2026-04-11 from parallel research across Brand New, It's Nice That, The Brand Identity, Creative Boom Hotlist 2025, Fonts In Use, and credits on SMASH's admired reference studios.

---

## Budget reality

**€4–7k for a brand refresh** (not a full rebrand). At this price, the realistic pool is:
- Solo designers and 2–3 person studios
- Rising names actively building portfolios
- Studios explicitly offering fixed-price refresh packages
- Top-tier designers willing to take the project at portfolio rates for SMASH's client roster (Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, Nobel Prize, Givenchy)

**Not realistic at this budget:** Pentagram, &Walsh, COLLINS, Mother Design, DIA, Order, Porto Rocha, Ragged Edge, SomeOne, Spin, Bureau Borsche, Studio Feixen, Bureau Mirko Borsche, Studio Dumbar, Kurppa Hosk / Heydays / Neue (now merged into a 120-person mega-agency), BVD, Stockholm Design Lab, Bold, ANTI, Leslie David, Mucho, Lobulo, Studio Moniker (closed 2023), Bunch Design (dormant).

**The force multiplier:** SMASH's client list is unusual for a 3-person studio. Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, Nobel Prize, Givenchy is the kind of roster that makes mid-tier designers take calls they'd normally pass on. Lead with it in every cold email.

## Strategic observations

1. **The Gentilhomme → Sepentzis credit is unusually valuable** because most creative-technology studios self-brand. There's almost no other path to "hire the exact designer who built a reference brand" in this space.

2. **The Nordic boutique market consolidated hard in 2024/2025.** Three of the names on an original "investigate" list merged into a 120-person mega-agency. This thins the Stockholm-local options significantly and makes Copenhagen, Berlin, and Amsterdam the strongest regional alternatives.

3. **SMASH's taste references are motion-rich and voice-forward.** The Nordic default is quieter and more minimal. Expect the best fit to come from Berlin, Amsterdam, London, or Montreal rather than Stockholm.

4. **Fixed-price is the clearest signal of budget-friendliness.** Duties.xyz and Twice Shy are the only candidates explicitly advertising scope-bounded pricing models. That's not a coincidence — it's how indie studios signal they'll take briefs without games.

5. **SMASH's client roster is a real force multiplier.** Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, Nobel Prize, Givenchy on a first cold email changes the response rate. Lead with it — specifically, lead with the "three-person Stockholm studio that's done work for X, Y, Z" frame.

6. **Pre-qualify each candidate's motion/kinetic capability.** The shattered-glass symbol will need to feel alive — it's not a static mark. Several candidates on this list are strong typographic designers but may not think natively in motion. Ask to see moving work before committing.
