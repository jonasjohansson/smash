# What the SMASH mark says

2026-10-13. Written after the Slant exploration (`docs/plans/2026-10-11-slant-design.md`, the branch `slant`), when
Jonas found the slant "not so timeless" and was "not sure what it communicates". A panel of four lenses (brand,
timeless wordmarks, space and light, custom type) wrote this brief; four judges then scored every version against
it, and the original mark came first (70 of 100; the slant 32). The decision: the original mark stays as it is.

## The mark

A solid block, a wall or a room, made open by slots of light. That is the studio's work in one shape: a fixed space
turned into a place people enter. The block reads as a building before it reads as letters.

- **A slot closed inside the block** is a window: it ends round, where the cutter stops.
- **A slot that runs out through an edge** is a threshold: it ends square, cut by the edge.
- **The S's quarter-circle turn** is a route through the building: round outside, sharp inside.
- **On ink**, the slots are light in a black box. Ink is the primary ground; paper is its negative.
- **The strict structure** (the grid of 4, bar 32, slot 20, pitch 52, two identical S's, each the same turned
  upside down) is the promise to deliver.

It is calm and upright: the grounded counterweight to a loud name, not an illustration of it. No blade, no speed,
no impact. It should look right routed into a lobby wall in 2046 and still read SMASH on a projection or a 16 px tab.

## How a version is judged

| Criterion | Weight | The question |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning: a space made open | 22 | Covered up, does it read as one solid volume with openings for light and people? Can each kind of opening be named (window, threshold, route)? |
| Timelessness | 20 | Could it have been made in 1975 and still be in use in 2046? Only verticals, horizontals and circular arcs; nothing that names its year (diagonal cuts, chamfers, blades, squircle corners, outer-box rounding, stencil slashes). |
| An authored irregularity, if any | 18 | Exactly one, with a one-sentence reason in the letter's anatomy, built from the system's own measures; never a melt, a drip, a flag or a misprint. |
| Legibility | 15 | SMASH at headline size, at 24 px, in the stack and in one colour; the A stays an A, the S an S. |
| Open and human | 13 | Soft where a person would meet it (round slot ends, round outsides of bends), with no acute angle; inviting, like light through slits in a dark room, never sporty or violent. |
| System and ownership | 12 | Every measure on the grid of 4, both S's identical, every lockup on the same rules, grown from the original rather than borrowed. |

## Variation belongs to the live mark

The still mark is frozen. What made CM.SUPPLY's version feel custom, and what the exploration enjoyed (slots
opening, angles, a letter settling), moves into the live mark, where it answers people and light:

- a closed slot may open through its edge as people approach (window to threshold);
- a letter may settle or lift with a crowd, and return at rest;
- a diagonal appears only as the angle of a passing light or body, never in the outline.

Every state returns to the still mark at rest. Nothing is randomised.
