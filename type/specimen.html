Notes on the letters

R and K

As narrow as the rest: R’s leg, and K’s arm and leg, meet the stem through a rounded notch. The first, wider R and K are in the font as stylistic set 1.

V, X, Q

No diagonals: V’s stems close in to a narrow foot, X is pinched in the middle, and Q’s tail goes down under the baseline, a rail deep, so Q is no wider than O.

O, D, 0

One tall counter; D has its right corners stepped in, 0 its opposite corners, as a hint of the slash.

1, 7, G, J

1 hangs a terminal from the top rail; 7 steps across the middle rail; J hooks at the foot. G breaks the grid a little: the end of its lower stroke turns in towards its back.
