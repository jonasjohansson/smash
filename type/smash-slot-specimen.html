SMASH Slot, a typeface drawn from the mark

Every letter is built the way the SMASH mark is: bars 31.8 units wide, slots 20 wide, three rails (top, middle, bottom), round ends where a counter closes against the top or bottom, flat against the middle, and the outer corner of every bend rounded. S, M, A and H are the logo’s own letters.

Choices to make

R and K

Two bars can’t give R its leg without it becoming the logo’s A, so R and K are three bars wide, the leg stepping out.

V, X, Q

No diagonals: V narrows in a step, X is two Ys (its joins sit in the thin 20-unit strips), Q’s tail runs out along the bottom rail.

O, D, 0

One tall counter; D has its right corners stepped in, 0 its opposite corners, as a hint of the slash.

1, 7, G, J

1 hangs a terminal from the top rail; 7 steps across the middle rail; G’s jaw rises to the middle; J hooks at the foot.
