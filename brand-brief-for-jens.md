## How SMASH should feel

Our space clusters around two poles: **corporate-impersonal** (Moment Factory) and **art-world-inaccessible** (teamLab, Random International). Neither is us.

**SMASH is:**
- Accessible confidence — serious craft, open door
- Warm, not cold — you'd want to work with us, not just admire us
- Technical and creative — not choosing between art and engineering
- Premium without being precious
- Punchy, not smooth
- Three visible humans, not an anonymous studio

**SMASH is not:** a tech company, a generic agency, an art collective too elevated to take a brief, corporate.

## Visual direction

Instincts, not decisions — open to your perspective:

- **Dark** — the work lives in dark rooms, projected onto buildings. Dark backgrounds make it glow.
- **Bold accent colours** — energetic, not muted. Current deck uses gold/yellow.
- **Dense, not airy** — more mission control than white cube.
- **Video-forward** — brand system should work with moving image.

**Tone of voice** the brand should support: clear, confident, warm. Punchy and declarative. Practitioner voice, not agency voice. No "we create experiences that inspire."

## Symbol concept: Shattered glass

A shattered/splintered glass mark. Dual meaning:
1. **The impact** — the name SMASH, the energy of collision
2. **The beauty** — light refracting through broken surfaces, connecting to projection/light work

The break IS the beauty.

**The goal:** The symbol should be able to stand completely on its own. Wordmark and symbol are both synonymous with SMASH — you see this abstract glass thing and you're like, "that's SMASH." Think Nike swoosh energy: the mark alone should carry the identity.

**How literal?** Open across the spectrum — from obvious radial impact to subtle angular geometry where you discover the glass reference. Rough SVG explorations attached (A–F) showing the range:

- **A.** Full radial impact — most literal, closest to original sketch
- **B.** Radial with S in negative space — hidden initial, just one idea
- **C.** Contained cluster — shards within a circle, icon-friendly
- **D.** Minimal crack — clean square, single fracture, most subtle
- **E.** Abstract shard — single fragment, most reduced
- **F.** Tessellation — angular facets filling a shape, crystalline

Colour could later enhance through refraction effects, gradients, or category colours in individual fragments. Needs to work at favicon scale (32px) through to large format.
