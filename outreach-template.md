# Designer Outreach Templates

Short, direct cold email templates for the brand designer shortlist. Keep them warm, honest, and specific. Lead with SMASH's credentials — they're the biggest advantage this list has at €4–7k.

**Rules of thumb:**
- Under 150 words. Designers at this level get a lot of cold email. Short wins.
- Subject line should do real work — it's the difference between open and archive.
- Link the brief (brand-brief-for-jens.md, once published somewhere), don't paste it.
- Name a specific project of theirs you liked. Generic praise reads as spam.
- Be honest about budget in the first email. Waste no one's time.

## Template 1 — Pre-qualified match (for Charlene Sepentzis)

Use when the designer has direct credit on a brand SMASH admires. Frame it as respect, not flattery. Address the potential conflict openly.

**Subject:** A question from a Stockholm studio in adjacent territory

> Hi Charlene,
>
> I run a studio called SMASH in Stockholm — three of us, making immersive installations, projection mapping, and stage design for clients like Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, and Nobel Prize. We admire what you did for Gentilhomme. It's the clearest contemporary expression of the register we want to live in.
>
> We're working on an identity refresh — keeping the equity in our current wordmark, evolving it, pairing it with a new symbol (shattered glass concept), typography recommendations, and colour direction. Scoped as a refresh, not a rebuild. Budget sits in the €4–7k range.
>
> Two questions:
>
> 1. Would the Gentilhomme work be a conflict from your side? We'd rather know now than later.
> 2. If not, is this a brief you'd be open to a first call about?
>
> Happy to send the full brief if there's interest.
>
> — Ash
> Founder & Creative Director, SMASH

## Template 2 — Standard first contact (Tier 1 and Tier 2)

For candidates who are a strong fit but don't have a direct reference credit. Lead with specificity, client roster, and clean scope.

**Subject:** Brand refresh brief — SMASH (Stockholm)

> Hi [First name],
>
> Ash here, co-founder of SMASH — a three-person studio in Stockholm making immersive installations, projection mapping, and stage design. Recent work has been for Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, Nobel Prize, Givenchy, and Spotify.
>
> I saw your work on [specific project — e.g. "the Tobias van Daal identity" / "Rally Festival" / "UNTO"] and it sits exactly in the register we're after: [one specific thing — e.g. "wordmark-led with real typographic conviction" / "confident without trend-chasing" / "bold but grounded"].
>
> We're looking to refresh our identity — evolve an existing wordmark that has equity, add a symbol (shattered glass concept), recommend typography, set colour direction, and provide light usage guidelines. Scope is a refresh, not a rebuild. Budget is €4–7k.
>
> If that's in the range of work you take on, I'd love to share the brief and see if there's a fit.
>
> — Ash
> smash.studio

## Template 3 — Stretch pick (Tier 3, likely above budget)

For designers who are above budget but worth asking. Be open about it. Frame the ask as "I know this is below your usual range, but here's why I'm asking."

**Subject:** A refresh brief — probably below your rate, worth asking anyway

> Hi [First name],
>
> This email has a self-aware opening: I know a €4–7k refresh is below what a studio your size usually engages on. I'm sending it anyway because the fit is real.
>
> I'm Ash, co-founder of SMASH — three people in Stockholm making immersive installations, projection mapping, and stage design. Clients include Netflix, HBO, Eurovision, Nobel Prize, Givenchy, and Spotify. Your work on [specific project — e.g. "Warp Records" / "HKW" / "the Sony Music estate identities"] is the closest thing we've seen to the register we want.
>
> The brief is a focused refresh: evolve an existing wordmark, add a symbol (shattered glass concept), recommend typography, set colour direction, deliver light guidelines. No brand book, no strategy overhaul. Clean scope.
>
> If this is a project you'd scope at that level — even a scoped-down version — I'd love to share the brief. If not, I'll take a "no" quickly and appreciate the honesty.
>
> — Ash
> smash.studio

## Subject line variations to test

- **Direct:** "Brand refresh brief — SMASH (Stockholm)"
- **Specific:** "A three-person Stockholm studio looking for a brand partner"
- **Credential-forward:** "Brand refresh for SMASH (Netflix, HBO, Eurovision)"
- **Curiosity:** "A question from a Stockholm studio in adjacent territory"
- **Honest:** "Probably below your rate — worth asking anyway"

Specificity beats cleverness. "Brand refresh brief — SMASH (Stockholm)" tells the recipient everything they need to open it: what it is, who it's from, where you are.

## Things to include as attachments or links

- `brand-brief-for-jens.md` — rename to `brand-brief.md` before sharing, or convert to PDF
- Selected Projects deck (PDF)
- Old site link
- Symbol explorations (A–F SVGs)

Don't attach all of this on first contact. Offer it. "Happy to send the full brief if there's interest" is better than dumping 6 MB on a stranger.

---

## What to do after sending

- Track replies in a simple table: name, date sent, replied Y/N, response summary, next step
- Give each cold email **5 working days** before moving on or sending a follow-up
- If someone says no but is warm about it, ask who they'd recommend — designers know each other
- If no one in Tier 1 responds in 10 days, move to Tier 2 and revisit Tier 1 with a gentle follow-up

---

## One thing to avoid

Don't mention Jens. Don't frame this as a backup search. It changes the power dynamic — designers read "we already have someone, we're just shopping" as uninterested and are less likely to engage. Treat every cold email as if they're your first choice, because they might become it.
