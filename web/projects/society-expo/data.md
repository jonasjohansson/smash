    content: Society Expo Exhibition takes place at [Skellefteå museum](https://skellefteamuseum.se/utstallning/society-expo-exhibition-2/) on Nordanå. Part of Society Expo 2026, the exhibition tells the story of Skellefteå's transformation through sound, light, projections and environments that respond to visitors. Four parts follow the energy of nature, people, society and the future.

  - type: text
    fontSize: small
    content: The journey begins with forest, rock and water. Visitors follow the river through a recreation of the Finnfors hydropower tunnel, where water becomes electricity. In this first room, light runs through real tree trunks. We cut the trees in half and left them to dry for a few months, then drilled a channel and fitted the LED cable inside. We used WS2815 LEDs at 96 LEDs per metre, QuinLED controllers and custom software to control the light.
