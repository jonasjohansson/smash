    content: "When Danne graduated, he had already caught the attention of leading choreographers and was headed for a career in dance. 19 years later, he was so affected by electroshock treatments and heavy medication that he could barely move. In 2018, Danne disappeared in Los Angeles. He was found surrounded by American police with drawn weapons. His bipolar disorder had taken hold again. In Jag är Gud, Danne looks back on his life: a youth as a promising dancer, his constant chase for love, parties with Russian oil billionaires and Spanish hitmen, his first manic episode in New York, and the stable years that could no longer keep the illness at bay."
