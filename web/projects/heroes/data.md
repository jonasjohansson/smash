    fontSize: small
    content: Heroes was a projection mapped piece on the facade of [Stockholms Stora Synagoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Synagogue_(Stockholm)) in 2022. The work honoured [Raoul Wallenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raoul_Wallenberg), who saved thousands of Jews in Budapest during the Holocaust, and [Dag Hammarskjöld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dag_Hammarskj%C3%B6ld), 1961 Nobel Peace Prize laureate and UN Secretary-General. Real-life statues of both men were 3D scanned and the synagogue was captured by drone, giving us precise geometry to work with. Black and white, high contrast, with tight animation set to the building's ornate architecture.
