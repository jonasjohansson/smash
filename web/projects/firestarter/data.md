    content: Firestarter is an interactive installation presented at [Nowhere](https://www.goingnowhere.org/) in Monegros, a regional burn I have attended since 2015. The piece is an enlarged replica of the classic Zippo lighter. I wanted to work with fire as a material, so we learned propane systems, sensors, and ignition triggers from the ground up. You walk up, you light a fire. A small, rebellious act of speaking up. It lit the official closing ceremony.
