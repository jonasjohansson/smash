The interactive installation premiered at [Tekniska museet during the 2025 Baltic Sea Festival](https://www.tekniskamuseet.se/pa-gang/tekniska-pa-ostersjofestivalen/), bringing the Swedish Radio Symphony Orchestra into Wisdome Stockholm.

  - type: text
    label: "How it works"
    fontSize: small
    content: "A camera tracks visitors' movements. KTH's machine learning model, trained on recordings of conductors, translates those gestures into tempo: move faster and the orchestra accelerates; pause and it waits. No suit or calibration is needed. KTH researchers Hedvig Kjellström and Mert Mermerci worked on the project, supported by WASP and SeRC."
  - type: image
    src: /projects/dome-conductor/development.jpg
    alt: A developer works at a laptop and a monitor showing the dome projection setup, with the orchestra projected in the background.
  - type: text
    label: "The experience"
    fontSize: small
    content: "The experience uses the opening ninety seconds of Beethoven's Fifth Symphony, recorded with conductor Daniel Harding. A wide-angle camera captured the orchestra for the dome's 180° projection, while playback lets the visitor set the pace. The musicians also recorded reactions: conduct them successfully and they applaud."
