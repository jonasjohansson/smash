We start from the building. A town hall, a synagogue, a five-storey facade, a stage: each piece is drawn to its surface's own lines, so the architecture becomes the story rather than a screen for it.

Then we open it to the people in front of it. They paint a facade in colour, chase celebrities through its windows, or watch four hundred years of a town's history pass over its walls.
