Installations that run live: cameras, sensors and real-time software that answer whoever steps up. In Kungsträdgården, a screen dropped passers-by into an alien invasion of Stockholm, day and night for two weeks. In Transcend, two strangers' hands met in mid-air inside a pyramid of glass.
