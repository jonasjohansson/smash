## Strategic objective

Position SMASH as a premium creative partner for interactive, immersive, and spatial experiences. Make it easy for the right people to understand why SMASH is different, trust the work, and reach out.

---

## Positioning

### Working positioning statement
*Not yet found. Needs a copywriter's touch, not more iteration.*

**Territory:** The collision between physical space and creative imagination. Real places transformed by art, technology, and nerve. Delivered by a small team with outsized ambition.

**Previous copy:** "A spectacular collision between the real and the imagination" — captures the spirit but needs more punch and declaration.

**What the line needs to do:** Be specific enough it couldn't be pasted onto Gentilhomme or Moment Factory. Own the tension between intimate team and ambitious scale. Feel like SMASH, not like a studio website template.

### What differentiation might look like (to be tested)
- Not a tech studio that makes pretty things
- Not a design agency that occasionally does spatial work
- A practice where artistic vision, spatial thinking, and public participation are inseparable from execution
- The combination is the differentiator — not any single element

## Target audiences

### Primary: People deciding whether to work with SMASH

**Commissioners and clients:** cultural institutions, museums, festivals, brands, place-makers, exhibition partners, property developers

What they likely need:
- quick understanding of what SMASH does
- confidence in creative quality *and* delivery capability
- relevant project examples
- a sense of fit — "these people understand our kind of challenge"

**Collaborators:** curators, agencies, producers, scenographers, architects, technology partners

What they likely need:
- understanding of SMASH's role and working style
- signals of artistic and technical fluency
- evidence of collaborative capability

### Secondary: People referencing SMASH
Press, event organizers, industry viewers — need concise framing and easy access to standout work.

## Messaging pillars

Consolidated from the original five to three that don't overlap:

### 1. Spatial transformation
SMASH changes how places feel — atmosphere, behavior, emotional response. The work is spatial, not just visual.

### 2. Meaningful participation
The work invites people in. Interaction is intuitive, playful, and emotionally rewarding — never gimmicky.

### 3. Vision through to delivery
SMASH holds artistic ambition and real-world execution together. Concept, design, storytelling, and production are one practice, not a pipeline.
